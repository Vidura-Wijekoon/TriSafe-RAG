# Passport — Issue and Renewal

## English

The Department of Immigration and Emigration issues passports to Sri Lankan
citizens. The printed application form K-35A is used for new and renewal
applications and can be collected from the Department or downloaded from the
website.

Processing fees:

- Normal service: LKR 10,000.00
- Urgent (one-day) service: LKR 20,000.00

The one-day service is only available at the Head Office in Battaramulla.
Applications for the one-day service are accepted between 7.00 a.m. and
1.30 p.m. on weekdays. Overseas applicants apply through Sri Lankan diplomatic
missions using the same K-35A form.

Required documents include the National Identity Card, the previous passport
(for renewals), and two recent passport-size photographs that meet the
prescribed specifications.

## தமிழ் (summary)

சாதாரண சேவைக்கான கட்டணம் ரூ. 10,000. அவசர (ஒரு நாள்) சேவைக்கான கட்டணம்
ரூ. 20,000. ஒரு நாள் சேவை பத்தரமுல்லை தலைமைச் செயலகத்தில் மட்டுமே
கிடைக்கும். K-35A படிவத்தைப் பயன்படுத்தவும்.
