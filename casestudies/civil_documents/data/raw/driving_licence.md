# Driving Licence — Charges for Services

## English (Schedule I — Levies for Normal and Same-Day Service)

New driving licence and learner's permit (Item 1):

- One class: Normal LKR 2500, Same-day LKR 3500
- Two classes at the same time: Normal LKR 3000, Same-day LKR 4000
- Three or more classes: Normal LKR 3500, Same-day LKR 4500

Renewal or extension of validity of a driving licence (Item 6):

- One period: Normal LKR 2800, Same-day LKR 4000
- Additional levy for a lapsed period of one year or less: LKR 500

Duplicate of a driving licence (Item 7):

- For the existing validity period: Normal LKR 2800, Same-day LKR 4000
- Due to loss of the driving licence: Normal LKR 7800, Same-day LKR 9000

Other charges:

- Re-applying for the driving licence written test: LKR 500
- Re-applying for the driving licence practical test: LKR 1000
- Changing particulars of a driving licence: LKR 3300 normal, LKR 4500 same-day
- International driving permit: LKR 3000 normal, LKR 3500 same-day
- Postal charges (packaging, delivery and courier): LKR 225
