# Antenatal care — schedule and structure

## English

Antenatal care should begin as soon as a pregnancy is suspected, preferably
before 12 weeks. In Sri Lanka antenatal care is delivered in two modalities:

- Domiciliary care provided by the area Public Health Midwife (PHM).
- Clinic care provided by Medical Officers.

A pregnant woman is expected to have at least 8 antenatal clinic visits with
a skilled healthcare provider and 3 or more home visits by the PHM during
the pregnancy.

The first antenatal clinic visit includes blood pressure measurement, urine
analysis, blood group testing, haemoglobin screening, and education on
nutrition, danger signs, and birth preparedness.

Iron and folic acid supplementation is provided to nearly all pregnant women
in Sri Lanka; a 2016 national survey found that 98 percent of women with a
recent live birth had taken iron pills or capsules during pregnancy.

## සිංහල (summary)

ගර්භණී සමයේ පළමු වෛද්‍ය පරීක්ෂාව සති 12 කට පෙර ආරම්භ කිරීම සුදුසු වේ.
ශ්‍රී ලංකාවේ පූර්ව ප්‍රසව සත්කාරය නිවසේ පෞද්ගලික සෞඛ්‍ය මාතාව (PHM)
මගින් සහ සායන වෛද්‍යවරයන් මගින් ලබා දේ. අවම වශයෙන් සායන 8 ක් සහ නිවස
තුළ 3 ක් හෝ ඊට වැඩි පරීක්ෂණ අවශ්‍ය වේ.

## தமிழ் (summary)

கர்ப்பத்தின் முதல் மருத்துவ பரிசோதனை 12 வாரங்களுக்கு முன்னர் தொடங்க
பரிந்துரைக்கப்படுகிறது. இலங்கையில் கர்ப்பக் கால பராமரிப்பு பொது சுகாதார
மருத்துவச்சி (PHM) மற்றும் மருத்துவ அதிகாரிகள் வழியாக வழங்கப்படுகிறது.
குறைந்தது 8 கிளினிக் சந்திப்புகள் மற்றும் 3 அல்லது அதற்கு மேற்பட்ட வீட்டு
சந்திப்புகள் அவசியம்.
