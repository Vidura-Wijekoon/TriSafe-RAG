# National Immunization Programme — schedule

## English

The Expanded Programme on Immunization (EPI) was launched in Sri Lanka in
1978. The Hib-containing pentavalent vaccine was introduced in 2008. The
programme is delivered free of charge through Medical Officer of Health
(MOH) clinics across the country.

Key milestones from the national immunization schedule:

- BCG vaccine: administered at 0 to 4 weeks of age, before the newborn
  leaves the hospital, often within 24 hours of birth.
- Pentavalent (DTP-HepB-Hib) vaccine: administered in the infancy series.
- MMR vaccine: a national MMR Catch-up Immunization Programme was launched
  on 6 January 2024 by the Epidemiology Unit, targeting children aged
  9 months to 15 years who missed their routine MMR doses.

A child should not be vaccinated when they have a fever above 38 degrees
Celsius on the day of the appointment. The vaccination should be rescheduled
with the MOH clinic. Mild low-grade fever and soreness at the injection site
within 24 to 48 hours are normal post-vaccination reactions.

## தமிழ் (summary)

இலங்கையின் தேசிய நோய்த்தடுப்பூசி திட்டம் (EPI) 1978 இல் தொடங்கப்பட்டது.
BCG தடுப்பூசி பிறப்பிலிருந்து 4 வாரங்களுக்குள் வழங்கப்படுகிறது. MMR Catch-up
திட்டம் 2024 ஜனவரி 6 இல் தொடங்கப்பட்டது. குழந்தைக்கு அந்த நாளில் 38°C க்கு
மேல் காய்ச்சல் இருந்தால் தடுப்பூசி வழங்கக் கூடாது.
