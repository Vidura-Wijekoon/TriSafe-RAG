# Seasonal influenza — public guidance

## English

Influenza is a viral infection. Antibiotics do not treat influenza. Misuse of
antibiotics for viral illnesses contributes to antimicrobial resistance, a
public-health concern that the Ministry of Health has highlighted in
successive advisories.

General precautions during flu season:

- Stay home if you have flu-like symptoms (fever, cough, sore throat).
- Cover coughs and sneezes with a tissue or the elbow.
- Wash hands with soap and water for at least 20 seconds, especially after
  coughing, sneezing, or touching shared surfaces.
- People in high-risk groups (children under 5, adults over 65, pregnant
  women, and people with chronic respiratory or metabolic conditions) should
  consider the seasonal influenza vaccine, which is available privately.

Seek medical attention if symptoms last longer than 7 days, if breathing
becomes difficult, or if a high fever persists beyond 3 days.
