# Dengue — patient guidance

## English

Recommended medication for fever and pain: use Paracetamol only, at the dose
prescribed by your doctor. Do NOT use Non-Steroidal Anti-Inflammatory Drugs
(NSAIDs) such as Ibuprofen, Aspirin, Diclofenac Sodium, Mefenamic Acid, or
Celecoxib (tablets or syrup) to reduce fever, because these drugs increase
the risk of bleeding.

Seek immediate medical attention if any warning signs appear:

- persistent vomiting,
- severe abdominal pain,
- bleeding from the gums, nose, or in stools,
- restlessness, lethargy, or cold extremities,
- a sudden drop in temperature accompanied by sweating.

Prevention focuses on eliminating Aedes mosquito breeding sites: empty and
scrub water-holding containers at least once a week, cover all stored water,
and dispose of discarded tyres, tins, and plastic items that collect
rainwater.

Home remedies (papaya leaf juice, lime juice, herbal preparations) are not
proven cures for dengue and must not replace medical care.
