# Cyclone and severe-weather preparedness

## English

The Department of Meteorology issues cyclone warnings and severe-weather
advisories for Sri Lanka. During cyclone preparedness in coastal districts,
households are advised to:

- Secure loose objects around the home and identify the nearest safe
  building or evacuation shelter.
- Store at least three days of drinking water per family member, and
  charge mobile phones and power banks once a warning is issued.
- Keep important documents in a waterproof container, and pack a small
  emergency kit (medication, identification copies, torch, first-aid).

During the cyclone passage:

- Stay indoors. Move to an interior room away from windows.
- The eye of the cyclone passing overhead can create a deceptive calm of
  about 20 to 30 minutes before high winds resume from the opposite
  direction. Do not go outside during this calm.

After the cyclone has passed, watch for downed power lines, broken glass,
and structurally damaged buildings. Downed lines should be reported to the
Ceylon Electricity Board.

## தமிழ் (summary)

வளிமண்டலவியல் திணைக்களம் (Department of Meteorology) புயல் எச்சரிக்கைகளை
வெளியிடுகிறது. ஒவ்வொரு குடும்ப உறுப்பினருக்கும் 3 நாட்களுக்கு போதுமான
குடிநீரை சேமிக்கவும். புயலின் கண் கடக்கும் போது 20–30 நிமிட அமைதியான
நேரம் இருக்கலாம்; வெளியே செல்ல வேண்டாம்.
