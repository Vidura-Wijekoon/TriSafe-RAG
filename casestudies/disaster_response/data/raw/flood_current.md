# Disaster Management Centre — emergency hotline and operations

## English

The Disaster Management Centre (DMC) has been allocated 117 as its
emergency telephone number. A dedicated telephone line was established for
the public to communicate with the DMC. The Call Centre is operating
24 hours a day, 7 days a week with 21 call operators.

During floods and other declared disasters, the DMC issues Situation
Reports through its public-facing reports portal. Citizens are advised to
check the DMC site for the current bulletin before relying on older
publications.

General flood safety advice issued by DMC and partner agencies:

- Do not wade through flowing water deeper than ankle depth on foot.
- Vehicles should not be driven through flooded roads; even six inches of
  fast-flowing water can sweep a car off the road.
- After flood waters recede, drinking water from open wells should be
  boiled for at least one minute or treated with chlorine tablets, because
  standing flood water often contains sewage and chemical runoff.
