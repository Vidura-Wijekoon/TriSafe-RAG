# NBRO landslide early-warning levels

## English

The National Building Research Organisation (NBRO) issues landslide early
warnings based on 24-hour rainfall thresholds.

- Yellow (Level 1) warning: when rainfall in the previous 24 hours exceeds
  75 mm, the public should be aware of the possibility of landslides,
  slope failures, rock falls, cut-slope failure, and ground subsidence,
  and should be prepared to evacuate to a safe location if necessary.

Special attention should be paid to all hilly areas, to man-made cut-slope
roads within the affected districts, and in particular to all landslide-prone
sites previously designated by NBRO.

Early warning signs that require immediate action include:

- new cracks in walls, floors, or the ground,
- doors or windows that suddenly start sticking,
- tilted trees or fences,
- water seeping from unusual locations on a slope.

If severe landslide signs are observed, the public should promptly inform
the relevant authorities and evacuate to a safer place to avoid the risks.

## සිංහල (summary)

ජාතික ගොඩනැගිලි පර්යේෂණ සංවිධානය (NBRO) පසුගිය පැය 24 ක වර්ෂාපතනය 75 mm
ඉක්මවන විට කහ (මට්ටම 1) භූමි කඩාවැටීම් අනතුරු ඇඟවීම නිකුත් කරයි.
නාය යාම් රෝග ලක්ෂණ දකින්නේ නම් වහා ආරක්ෂිත ස්ථානයකට ඉවත් වන්න.

## தமிழ் (summary)

தேசிய கட்டிட ஆராய்ச்சி ஸ்தாபனம் (NBRO) கடந்த 24 மணி நேரத்தில் மழைப்பொழிவு
75 மிமீ ஐ தாண்டும்போது மஞ்சள் (நிலை 1) நிலச்சரிவு எச்சரிக்கையை வெளியிடுகிறது.
கடுமையான நிலச்சரிவு அறிகுறிகள் தென்பட்டால் உடனடியாக பாதுகாப்பான இடத்திற்கு
வெளியேறவும்.
